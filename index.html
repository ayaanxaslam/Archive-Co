03 — The Story

Second-hand designer,
treated with care

Second-hand designer stuff, treated with care. That's the whole idea, and it's harder to find than it should be.

Most resale treats a garment like a listing — photographed on a bedroom floor, shipped in a bin bag, described in three words. We think a piece that survived a decade deserves better than that on its way to the next person.

So everything that comes through here gets the same treatment: checked over seam by seam, cleaned properly, repaired where it's honest to repair, photographed in real light, and written up with the flaws named out loud.

04 — How It Works

Before it reaches you

/ 01

Sourced

Pulled from archives, private wardrobes and long-standing contacts. We buy what we'd wear ourselves, not what's trending this week.

/ 02

Authenticated

Tags, stitching, hardware, construction, labelling. If a piece can't be verified with confidence, it doesn't go on the rail.

/ 03

Restored

Professionally cleaned, gently repaired where it's honest to do so. Never over-restored — age is part of the value.

/ 04

Documented

Shot in natural light, measured, graded and written up. Every flaw is named. No surprises when the box opens.
