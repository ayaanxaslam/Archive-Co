Full terms

The detail

Almost everything. Tell us within 14 days of delivery that you want to return a piece, then post it back within 14 days of that message.
  • It must come back unworn, unwashed and unaltered.
  • All tags, dust bags, boxes and extras included.
  • Sneakers must be tried on indoors only — soles clean, no outdoor wear.
  • Original packaging kept intact where practical.
  • Pierced jewellery and earrings — hygiene.
  • Pieces altered or tailored after delivery, including resizing.
  • Items returned worn, scented, marked or with sole wear.
  • Documented flaws. Every flaw is photographed and written into the listing before you buy. A return can't be raised for wear that was disclosed — that's what pre-owned means, and it's priced in.
  • Anything sold as final sale, which is labelled clearly on the listing.
  • Email ayaanxaslam@gmail.com with your order number and the reason.
  • We reply within 24 hours with a return reference and the address.
  • Pack it as it arrived — protected, boxed, not crushed into a bag.
  • Send it tracked. Until it reaches us, it's your responsibility, so keep the receipt.
Returns sent without a reference can't be matched to an order and may be sent back to you.
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 working days of the piece arriving and passing inspection. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.
  • Original shipping is refunded only if the piece was misdescribed or faulty.
  • Return postage is yours unless the fault was ours.
  • Exchanges aren't possible — every piece is one of one. Return it and buy the other piece separately.
  • Store credit is available instead of a refund and never expires.
This shouldn't happen — but if it does, it's on us. Send photos within 48 hours of delivery and we'll cover return postage and refund you in full, including original shipping. Same applies to anything damaged in transit or, in the extremely unlikely event, failing authentication elsewhere.
Everything ships tracked and insured, wrapped and boxed properly.
  • Dubai & UAE — 1–2 working days, tracked. Free over £150.
  • GCC — 2–4 working days, tracked and signed.
  • Rest of world — 5–10 working days, tracked and signed.
  • Orders are dispatched within 48 hours of clearing, Sunday to Thursday.
  • Import duties and taxes outside the UAE are the buyer's responsibility.
Nothing on this page reduces the rights you have as a consumer under the law that applies to you. Archive & Co trades from Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Where your local consumer law gives you more than this policy does, your local law wins; where our policy is the more generous of the two, our policy applies.
